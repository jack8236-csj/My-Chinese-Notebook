How to practise
  1. Listen, then say the word out loud.
  2. Do Trace, then Copy, then From memory.
  3. One character fills one square. Keep it centred and write the strokes in the right order.
Stroke order rules
  • 十shíHorizontal before vertical
  • 人rénLeft-falling before right-falling
  • 三sānTop to bottom
  • 川chuānLeft to right
  • 小xiǎoMiddle before the sides
  • 月yuèOutside before inside
  • 国guóWalls first, inside next, close the box last

Tone colours: 1st high flat · 2nd rising · 3rd dipping · 4th falling · neutral
